## 2. Purpose, audience, problem, and value

EcoLearn is an educational sustainability app for students, families, educators,
and community groups in the United States. It addresses two common problems:
people are often unsure how to dispose of a specific item, and sustainability
education is difficult to turn into repeatable action. EcoLearn combines
photo/manual item identification, source-linked Delaware disposal guidance,
nearby disposal-site discovery, short lessons and knowledge checks, progress and
badges, and private teacher-managed classrooms and community spaces. It is an
independent educational product and is not an official government app.

## 3. Setup and access to main features

No sample files, hardware accessories, subscriptions, or purchases are required.
Use the dedicated reviewer credentials in the App Review Sign-In Information
fields. That account is email-confirmed and has administrator access with private
demonstration data.

- **Home:** progress, XP, lessons, and shortcuts.
- **Scan:** choose Camera or Gallery, or type an item name. A verified match shows
  source-linked disposal guidance and can open Map with the item prefilled.
- **Map:** choose a service and tap Find nearby locations. Location permission is
  optional and requested only for this action.
- **Learn:** open a lesson, advance through its cards, and complete the knowledge
  check.
- **Community:** open the demonstration school and classrooms to see aggregate
  class standings, assignments, teacher/manager controls, announcements, and
  events. On another member's announcement/event, tap Report or Block. The admin
  account can review reports under Safety & moderation and remove or dismiss the
  reported content.
- **Profile:** edit the display name, open support/privacy/terms, sign out, or use
  Delete account. Deletion is permanent and requires two confirmations.

Camera, photo-library, and location access are optional. Manual search and
lessons remain available when permissions are denied.

## 4. External services, tools, and platforms

- **Supabase:** authentication, database, storage, server functions, and account
  deletion.
- **Apple Sign in with Apple and Google OAuth:** optional authentication methods;
  email/password authentication is also available.
- **OpenRouter and the configured vision model:** item identification from a
  user-selected image. EcoLearn does not retain scanner images or use them for
  model training.
- **Delaware DNREC Recyclopedia:** official disposal guidance and source links.
- **Delaware Solid Waste Authority (DSWA):** public facility information and
  educational resources.
- **Apple Maps/MapKit and OpenStreetMap:** map display, place context, and nearby
  disposal-site discovery.
- **Open Food Facts:** barcode product lookup when a barcode is used.
- **YouTube:** embedded or linked educational videos.

EcoLearn has no advertising, payment processor, in-app purchases, or external
social-media/messaging integration.

## 5. Regional differences

The app is offered only in the United States for this release. Authentication,
learning, scanning, progress, account management, community/classroom, reporting,
blocking, and moderation features function consistently for U.S. users. Disposal
rules and the curated nearby-facility dataset are Delaware-first, so official
DNREC/DSWA guidance and the most complete location coverage are specific to
Delaware. The interface clearly identifies and links those sources rather than
presenting Delaware rules as nationwide requirements.

## 6. Regulation and third-party material

EcoLearn does not provide medical, financial, legal, transportation, or other
regulated professional services. It is an independent educational product. The
developer confirms that EcoLearn has the necessary rights or permission to
display or access its DNREC, DSWA, YouTube, map, OpenStreetMap, Open Food Facts,
and other third-party content. Government and facility information is
source-attributed and linked so users can verify current rules, accepted
materials, locations, and hours. Map/open-data attribution is displayed in the
app, and YouTube content is embedded or linked through the platform's supported
mechanisms. No third-party credentials are required for App Review.
